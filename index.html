About

Agabode Solutions LLC is a residential housing development company dedicated to creating affordable, safe, and community-centered housing solutions for seniors (55+). The organization will acquire, renovate, develop, and manage residential properties and tiny home communities designed specifically to support aging adults who desire independence, affordability, and a strong sense of community. The initial phase of the business will focus on acquiring and renovating residential properties to serve as affordable senior housing. Long-term growth will include developing purpose-built tiny home communities and mixed-income senior housing neighborhoods throughout California and Nevada.

Our Mission

To restore dignity and respect to senior community members by providing housing and stability through excellence, compassion, and affordability.

Our Vision

To be the leading provider in building housing communities in which seniors can feel safe, secure, and comfortable
